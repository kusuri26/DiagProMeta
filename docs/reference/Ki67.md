# Meta-analysis of prognostic studies: Ki-67 data

A dataset from a meta-analysis of prognostic studies evaluating the
prognostic value of Ki-67 expression in early breast cancer. The dataset
contains study-level overall survival, hazard-ratio, and median
survival-time data.

## Usage

``` r
Ki67
```

## Format

A list of 3 datasets:

- OS:

  Dataset containing overall survival data. It includes `author` (study
  author), `study` (study identifier), `t` (follow-up time in month),
  `n1` and `n0` (sample sizes in the two comparison groups), and `s1`
  and `s0` (survival probabilities in the two groups).

- HR:

  Dataset containing hazard-ratio data. It includes `author` (study
  author), `study` (study identifier), `HR` (hazard ratio), and `ci.low`
  and `ci.up` (lower and upper confidence limits of the hazard ratio).

- mct:

  Dataset containing median follow-up time data. It includes `author`
  (study author), `study` (study identifier), `n1` and `n0` (sample
  sizes in the two comparison groups), `mct_mo` (median follow-up time
  in months), `s1_mct` and `s0_mct` (survival probabilities at median
  follow-up time in the two comparison groups).

## Source

de Azambuja E, Cardoso F, de Castro G, Colozza M, Mano MS, Durbecq V,
Sotiriou C, Larsimont D, Piccart-Gebhart MJ, Paesmans M (2007). Ki-67 as
prognostic marker in early breast cancer: a meta-analysis of published
studies involving 12 155 patients. *British Journal of Cancer*, 96(10),
1504–1513.
[doi:10.1038/sj.bjc.6603756](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bjc.6603756)
